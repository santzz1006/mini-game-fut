Biggest World Cup Final Crowd

The 1950 final between Brazil and Uruguay at the Maracana drew an estimated 199,854 people — still the largest attendance in football history. Brazil lost 2-1 in what Brazilians call the "Maracanazo".

Fastest Goal Ever Recorded

Nawaf Al-Abed scored just 2.4 seconds into a Saudi league match in 2009. He launched the ball from the kickoff straight into the net — before the opponent even touched it.

Most World Cup Titles

Brazil is the only nation to have won 5 World Cups (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002) and the only one to participate in every single edition of the tournament since 1930.

Biggest Ever Scoreline

AS Adema beat SO l'Emyrne 149-0 in a 2002 Madagascar league match. The losing side deliberately scored own goals in protest of a refereeing decision in a prior game.

Youngest World Cup Scorer

Pelé was just 17 years and 239 days old when he scored at the 1958 World Cup in Sweden — becoming the youngest goalscorer in World Cup final history, a record that still stands.

Most Club Goals in History

Josef Bican is credited with over 800 official goals, though records of his era are incomplete. Among modern players, Cristiano Ronaldo leads with 900+ goals across all competitions.
